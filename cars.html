Repair Assistance Program

Your car is broken and you can't afford to fix it.

We hear this constantly. You own a vehicle — it's sitting in your driveway with a dead alternator or a broken timing belt — and without it you can't get to work.

Apply for our repair assistance program. Certified mechanics volunteer their labour, and we coordinate the repair through our partner shop, Effective Autobody.

Apply for Repair Help →
Free labour
All mechanical work is done by certified professionals who volunteer their time.
Need-based priority
Families who rely on the vehicle for work or medical appointments are prioritized first.
BC residents only
Vehicle must be registered in your name in British Columbia.
