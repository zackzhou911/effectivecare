Applying for a Vehicle

No cost to apply. No cost to receive. A fair, transparent process open to anyone who demonstrates genuine need.

$0
Cost to apply or receive
The Recipient Journey

From application to keys in hand.

Repair Assistance: If you already own a vehicle that needs repairs you cannot afford, you may qualify for our Repair Assistance program — certified volunteer mechanics cover the labour, and parts are supplied at cost where possible. The vehicle must be registered in your name. Apply for Repair Help →

1
Am I eligible?

Applications are open to Metro Vancouver residents who demonstrate genuine need. Valid BC driver's licence, BC address, and the ability to operate a vehicle. We do not discriminate based on immigration status, past legal history, or credit score.

2
What does it cost?

Nothing. No application fee, no deposit, no cost to receive the vehicle. Effective Care covers inspection, certification, repairs, and ICBC title transfer. After receiving the vehicle, you are responsible for your own insurance, registration, fuel, and ongoing maintenance — the same as any car owner.

3
How do I apply?

Create an account in our portal and select "Recipient" as your account type. Fill out the application form — it takes about 10 minutes and asks about your household situation, employment, commute needs, and any special circumstances. All information is kept strictly confidential.

4
How am I selected?

Every application is reviewed against the same consistent set of factors: household income, number of dependents, employment situation, distance to work or school, housing stability, and special circumstances. Applications are not reviewed first-come-first-served — the family with the most demonstrated need is matched first.

5
How am I matched to a specific vehicle?

Once a vehicle is certified, we match it to the highest-need applicant whose profile is compatible — for example, a family with children may be matched with a vehicle that has appropriate seating capacity. You will be notified through your portal when a match is made.

6
What happens if I am approved?

We arrange a time for you to receive your certified vehicle at our partner shop at no cost. Title is transferred to you through ICBC at that time. Where possible, we offer a free mechanical basics session. You will be asked whether you consent to sharing anonymized updates with the donor — this is completely optional.

Not approved? We will explain why and you are welcome to reapply if your circumstances change. We keep all applications on file and may reach out if a vehicle becomes a strong match for your situation.
