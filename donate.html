Donate a Vehicle

Every car has a next chapter. Yours could change a family's life.

What Your Donation Does

One car. One family. Real change.

Your donated vehicle goes through a full inspection and certification at our partner shop, Effective Autobody. If it passes, it is matched to the BC family with the most demonstrated need. If it cannot be certified, it is sold for parts and the proceeds fund repairs for other vehicles. Either way, your donation moves someone forward.

$0
Cost to you

How To Donate

Simple from start to finish.

01

Contact us

Email info@effectivecare.ca to arrange a drop-off time. Tell us the make, model, year, and condition of your vehicle.

02

Prepare for drop-off

Bring your vehicle registration (in your name), a valid BC photo ID, and the keys. Remove your licence plates and cancel your Autoplan insurance before drop-off. If your vehicle is not running, contact us to discuss towing options.

03

Drop off and we handle the rest

We complete the ICBC ownership transfer on site. Once our CRA registration is confirmed, we will issue official donation receipts for eligible gifts. We take it from there.

04

Stay connected

Once your vehicle is placed, your "See Your Car" dashboard activates. With the family's consent, you receive anonymized updates showing how your car is still moving someone forward.
