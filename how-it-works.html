How It Works

Two journeys. One mission. From donation to key handover.

Overview

One chain. Two sides.

Every placement requires two people: someone willing to give, and someone ready to receive. Here is what both journeys look like — and how they connect.

Donor Journey

What happens when you donate a vehicle.

01

Contact us to arrange a drop-off

Email info@effectivecare.ca to set up a time at our partner shop. Bring your vehicle registration (in your name), a valid BC photo ID, and the keys. We handle the ICBC ownership transfer on site.

✓
Lien check
Before your vehicle is accepted, we verify that it has no outstanding financial lien. If a lien exists, it must be fully resolved before the donation can proceed.
✓
Tow truck pickup (where available)
If your vehicle cannot be driven, contact us and we will discuss towing options; pickup depends on location and availability.
02

We inspect and assess your vehicle

Our partner shop performs a full mechanical inspection. If the vehicle passes a road-safety inspection, we proceed to repairs. If it cannot, it is sold for parts — and the proceeds fund repairs for other vehicles in the pipeline.

03

Your donation is confirmed

We confirm your donation by email. Effective Care Society has applied for CRA charitable registration. Charity Registration Pending. Effective Care Society is not yet a registered charity and cannot issue official donation receipts. If our CRA registration is approved, receipts may be available for eligible gifts made after that date.

04

Your vehicle is matched to a family

Once certified, your vehicle is matched to the applicant with the most demonstrated need. You are notified when your car finds a home. Drop-off to placement typically takes two to four weeks.

05

Your "See Your Car" dashboard activates

Within 30 days of placement, we activate a private dashboard just for you. With the family’s consent, you receive anonymized updates — trips taken, jobs obtained, milestones reached, and messages of gratitude. No names or addresses are ever shared.

Donate a VehicleDonor FAQ

Getting Help

We help in two ways — a car, or a repair.

Whether you need a vehicle from scratch or just need yours fixed, the process starts the same way: a short application through our portal.

Need a vehicle

You don’t have a car.

We match you with a donated, certified vehicle — at no cost to you. You keep the car. No rental, no return, no strings.

Need a repair

You have a car. It just doesn’t run.

Labour is free; parts are supplied at cost. Bring it in; leave with it running.

The journey

01

Check if you qualify

Applicants must be Metro Vancouver residents. Valid BC driver’s licence, BC address, and the ability to operate a vehicle. We look at household income, number of dependents, and how much you rely on a vehicle day-to-day — for work, medical appointments, or caring for family. We don’t consider immigration status, credit score, or past legal history.

02

Apply through the portal

Create a "Recipient" account and choose the type of help you need: a vehicle, or Repair Assistance. The application takes about 10 minutes and is completely confidential. You can save and return to it at any time.

03

We review your application

Applications are reviewed based on need and, for repairs, shop capacity. We review applications in cycles and will respond as soon as a decision is made. We’ll contact you through your portal. If we’re full, you go on a waitlist and are notified when space opens.

04

We arrange everything at the shop

If you’re receiving a vehicle, we match you with one and arrange handover at Effective Autobody — ICBC paperwork included, no cost. If you need a repair, drop off your car; our mechanics assess and fix it. Labour is free; parts are supplied at cost.

05

You leave with a working car

That’s the whole point. After that, the car is yours to maintain — insurance, fuel, and registration are your responsibility, the same as any car owner.

What makes us different.

Our program does not end at drop-off. With the family’s opt-in consent, donors receive anonymized updates after placement — trips taken, jobs obtained, milestones reached. No names. No addresses. Just the knowledge that your car is still keeping someone moving.
