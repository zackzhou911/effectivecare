For Applicants

Questions applicants actually ask.

Yes. The application is free and takes about 10 minutes. There is no minimum income and we do not disqualify based on immigration status, credit history, or past legal issues. If you have genuine need and a valid BC driver's licence, apply.
Nothing to apply. Nothing to receive the vehicle. Effective Care covers inspection, certification, repairs, and the ICBC title transfer. After you receive it, you are responsible for your own insurance, registration, fuel, and maintenance - the same as any car owner. The car is free; running it is your responsibility.
Every application is reviewed against the same set of factors: household income, number of dependents, employment situation, distance to work or school, housing stability, and special circumstances. It is not first-come-first-served. The family with the most demonstrated need for a given vehicle is matched first.
Applications submitted by March 2027 will receive a response within the first review cycle (approximately 6 months from submission). We process applications in waves as vehicles become available and review staffing allows. If you applied and have not heard back within 6 months, feel free to follow up at info@effectivecare.ca.
You are welcome to reapply if your circumstances change. We keep all applications on file and may reach out if a vehicle becomes available that is a strong match for your situation.
No. Sharing updates through the "See Your Car" program is completely optional. Your identity is never revealed - if you do share, the donor receives only anonymized information. There is no pressure either way.
