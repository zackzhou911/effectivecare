Repair Assistance

You already have a car. It just doesn’t run. We help you get it back on the road.

Who it is for

Metro Vancouver drivers who can’t afford the repair bill.

A brake job, an alternator, a timing belt — the kind of repair that sits between you and your job, your kids’ school, or a medical appointment. Volunteer mechanics at our partner shop cover the labour. You pay only for parts, at cost.

To qualify you need: a vehicle registered in your name, a valid BC driver’s licence, a Metro Vancouver address, and a demonstrated need (work, school, caregiving, or medical travel). No credit check, no immigration status requirement, no past legal history disqualifier.

What is covered

Covered
Diagnostic, mechanical and safety repairs performed by volunteer mechanics: brakes, suspension, charging and starting systems, belts, hoses, fluids, lights, and similar.
You pay
Parts at cost. We tell you the parts estimate before any work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead.
Not covered
Collision and body work, cosmetic repairs, aftermarket upgrades, and vehicles that cannot be made road-safe within a reasonable parts budget.

How it works

Four steps.

01

Apply in the portal

Create a Recipient account, choose Repair Assistance, and tell us what is wrong with the car. It takes about ten minutes and is confidential.

02

We review and schedule

We review applications in cycles and will respond as soon as a decision is made. If the shop is full, you go on a waitlist and we contact you through your portal when a slot opens.

03

Drop off and get an estimate

Bring the car to our partner shop. Mechanics diagnose it and give you a parts-only estimate. Nothing is done until you approve it.

04

Drive home

Labour is free; parts are supplied at cost. You leave with a working car. Insurance, fuel and registration stay your responsibility, the same as any owner.
