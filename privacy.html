Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

Contact details, vehicle donation information, application data (household situation, employment, commute, housing), and portal account information provided directly by you.

How We Use It

To process donations and issue tax receipts, review and score applications, match vehicles to families, and communicate with donors and applicants about their submissions.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Recipient information is never shared with donors. "See Your Car" updates are always anonymized and always opt-in.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting info@effectivecare.ca. We respond within 30 days.
