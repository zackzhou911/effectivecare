Partnership Opportunities

Five ways to partner with us.

Repair Partner

Mechanic shops and collision centres that provide inspection, repair, or certification services. At-cost or donated-labour arrangements welcome.

Parts Partner

Auto parts suppliers or salvage yards that donate components toward repairing vehicles in our pipeline.

Fleet Partner

Companies upgrading their fleet can donate retired vehicles. We handle inspection, certification, and placement. Effective Care Society is not yet a registered charity and cannot issue official donation receipts. If our CRA registration is approved, receipts may be available for eligible gifts made after that date.

Financial Sponsor

Monetary contributions that fund repair costs and placement expenses.

Community Partner

Non-profits, social services, and employers that refer qualifying families or help identify donor vehicles in the community.

Want to sponsor?

We welcome shops, businesses, and individuals who want to support the community. Reach out and we will find a way to work together.
