Donating a Vehicle

Everything you need to know — from eligibility to tax receipts to staying connected after drop-off.

$0
Cost to the donor
Why Donate With Effective Care?

More than a drop-off. A lasting connection.

Most vehicle donation programs take your car and send you a receipt. Effective Care does something different. With the recipient's consent, you receive a private dashboard showing anonymized updates — miles driven, life milestones, and messages of gratitude from the family your car reached.

Your donation stays local. Every vehicle goes to a Metro Vancouver family. Every dollar from parts sales funds repairs for other placements. Nothing leaves the chain.

The Donor Journey

1
Why should I donate my car?

A vehicle that has outlived its usefulness for you can be the most important thing a Metro Vancouver family receives this year. Our program also keeps you connected to the outcome through your private dashboard. Effective Care Society is not yet a registered charity and cannot issue official donation receipts. If our CRA registration is approved, receipts may be available for eligible gifts made after that date.

2
Is my car eligible?

We accept cars, trucks, SUVs, and vans of any make, model, or year — running or not. You must be the registered owner. If the vehicle cannot be certified after inspection, it is sold for parts and proceeds fund repairs for other donations.

3
What documents do I need?

You will need the vehicle registration in your name, a valid BC photo ID, and the keys. If you have a lien on the vehicle, it must be paid off before donation. We handle the ICBC ownership transfer paperwork at drop-off.

4
What happens to my car?

Your vehicle goes to Effective Autobody for a full mechanical inspection. If it passes, it is repaired and matched to a qualifying family. Your "See Your Car" dashboard activates within 30 days with the family's consent.

5
What do I receive?

Effective Care Society is not yet a registered charity and cannot issue official donation receipts. If our CRA registration is approved, receipts may be available for eligible gifts made after that date. You also receive access to your private "See Your Car" dashboard once your vehicle is placed.

6
Licence plates and insurance

Remove your licence plates and cancel your Autoplan insurance before drop-off. Return the plates to ICBC. You are responsible for cancelling your own insurance. We handle the ownership transfer at drop-off.

7
How do I get started?

Contact us at info@effectivecare.ca to arrange a drop-off time. If your vehicle cannot be driven, contact us and we will discuss towing options; pickup depends on location and availability. The process from drop-off to placement typically takes two to four weeks.

Ready to donate?

Contact us to arrange a drop-off. We will confirm availability and walk you through the paperwork.
