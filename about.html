Our Story

It started with a shop, a family, and a simple question.

Our founders watched families in their community struggle to get to work, to appointments, to school. Not because they lacked the will — because they lacked the means. Meanwhile, cars sat unused in driveways, traded in, or scrapped.

The question was simple: what if we connected those two things?

Effective Care is the answer. A non-profit society rooted in a family collision repair shop in British Columbia, connecting donated vehicles with the families who need them most. Effective Care Society has applied for CRA charitable registration. Charity Registration Pending.
