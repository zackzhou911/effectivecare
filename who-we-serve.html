Who We Serve

Applications are open to anyone who qualifies through our transparent, need-based scoring process.

Eligibility

Open to anyone who needs it most.

No credit check, no immigration status requirement, no past legal history disqualifier. Only demonstrable need. Valid BC driver's licence, BC address, and the ability to operate a vehicle. Applicants must be Metro Vancouver residents.

Repair Assistance: If you already own a vehicle that needs repairs you cannot afford, you may qualify for our Repair Assistance program — volunteer mechanics cover the labour. Labour is free; parts are supplied at cost. The vehicle must be registered in your name. Apply for Repair Help →

Low-income working families
Parents with stable income or active employment who lack access to a reliable vehicle for their daily commute and family responsibilities.
Single parents
Primary caregivers navigating work, school runs, and appointments as the sole provider. Transportation is the difference between managing and struggling.
Transitioning out of homelessness
Individuals with a new verified address who need transportation to build stability and pursue employment consistently.
Veterans and disabled individuals
Those facing physical or economic barriers to vehicle ownership, for whom a car represents meaningful independence.
The Need Score

How we decide who gets a car.

Every applicant is scored across six weighted factors. The highest-scoring compatible applicant is matched to the available vehicle.

Household income (25%)
Measured as a percentage of the area median income. Lower income equals a higher score.
Number of dependents (20%)
Children and other dependents in the household who rely on the applicant for transportation.
Employment status (20%)
Active employment or verified job search activity. A car is most impactful when it enables work.
Commute need (15%)
Distance to workplace or school versus available transit. Higher distance with poor transit equals a higher score.
Housing stability (10%)
A current address where we can reach you. Recently housed applicants are welcome.
Special status (10%)
Veteran status, disability, or transition out of homelessness within the past 12 months.
Apply

Think you qualify?

There is no cost to apply and no obligation. We review applications in cycles and will respond as soon as a decision is made. All information is kept strictly confidential.
